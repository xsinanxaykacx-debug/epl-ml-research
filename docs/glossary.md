# Glossary

- OOS: Out-of-Sample; a period not used for model or parameter selection.
- Walk-forward: chronological train/test evaluation respecting time order.
- LogLoss: probabilistic scoring rule penalizing incorrect confident probabilities.
- Brier score: squared-error score for probabilistic classification.
- Overround: sum of inverse bookmaker odds before normalization minus 1.
- EV: expected value estimate used here to rank potential bets.
- Shrinkage: moving model probabilities toward a reference distribution, here the market.
- Divergence: difference between model and market probabilities.
- Calibration: agreement between predicted probabilities and observed frequencies.
- OOS edge: an advantage that survives a locked future test under the predefined protocol.
